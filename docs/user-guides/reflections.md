# Reflections

## Evening ritual

1. From Planner, tap **Start Reflection** (or **View reflection** if you already finished).
2. Write **what you learned** and **celebrations & wins**.
3. Review **Themes**. Presets (Focus, Rest, Gratitude, and the rest of the catalog) are there from the first evening. Slow Journey also suggests chips from your words. Tap to keep them, reuse tags from earlier evenings, or tap **Add theme** for a name of your own. Custom themes show up again next time.
4. Choose a **gratitude** face (gentler to brighter).
5. Optionally add a title. Tap **Add a photo** (or the camera icon) to choose a picture, then crop it. The cropper opens at **16:9** so it matches Feed journal cards; you can switch to **4:3**, **square**, or the original shape. Photos stay on this device. Tap a saved photo to view it full screen, pinch to zoom, recrop, or change it.
6. Tap **Complete Day**. The form fades into a full rest screen with your streak. Tap **Continue** to return.

One reflection per calendar day. Completing the day updates your streak.

## Journey feed

Open **Feed** to scroll past days: a 16:9 photo (tap to view full screen), title, the learning you saved, and theme chips. Each card names **Today**, **Yesterday**, or the weekday and date. Tap a card to reopen the evening form and edit themes. The header shows a sage streak badge with the current count.
