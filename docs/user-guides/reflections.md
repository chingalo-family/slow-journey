# Reflections

## Evening ritual

1. From Planner, tap **Start Reflection** (or **View reflection** if you already finished).
2. Write **what you learned** and **celebrations & wins**.
3. Choose a **gratitude** face (gentler to brighter).
4. Optionally add a title. Tap **Add a photo** (or the camera icon) to choose a picture, then crop it. The cropper opens at **16:9** so it matches Feed journal cards; you can switch to **4:3**, **square**, or the original shape. Photos stay on this device. Tap a saved photo to view it full screen, pinch to zoom, recrop, or change it.
5. Tap **Complete Day**. The form fades into a full rest screen with your streak. Tap **Continue** to return.

One reflection per calendar day. Completing the day updates your streak.

## Journey feed

Open **Feed** to scroll past days: a 16:9 photo (tap to view full screen), title, and the learning you saved. Each card names **Today**, **Yesterday**, or the weekday and date. The header shows a sage streak badge with the current count.
