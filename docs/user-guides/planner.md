# Planner

The planner is home. A **next step** card names the one thing to do (set intentions, live the day, or close the evening). Completed days show a sage dot.

## Move through weeks and months

1. Open **Planner**.
2. Stay on **Week** to step with the arrows, or switch to **Month** to see a full calendar.
3. Tap a date, or tap the period title (or the calendar icon) to **choose a date**.
4. **Today** returns you to the current day when you have moved away.

You can open past weeks and months to add or change intentions, tick what you honored, or close a missed evening. On a past day, both **Set intentions** and **Start Reflection** stay available at any time of day. Future days stay waiting until they arrive.

## Set intentions

1. Open **Planner**, or use **Today's Intentions** after you open and unlock the app when today has no intentions. You can set them any time that day. The greeting matches morning, afternoon, or evening.
2. Write up to **three** intentions for the day you are viewing.
3. Tap **Set My Day**.

You can change them later the same day. Empty slots are ignored.

## During the day

- Tick an intention when you have honored it. Incomplete items do not punish you.
- **Today's Wisdom** is a local quote — it does not need the internet.
- **Start Reflection** for today stays closed through the morning and afternoon. It opens after **5:00 PM**. If you already closed today, **View reflection** stays available.
- On a past day, **Set intentions** and **Start Reflection** (or **View reflection**) are both available.

If a day has no intentions yet, use **Set intentions** from the empty state.
