# Settings and account

There is no cloud account in Phase 1. **Setup** is this device only.

## Profile

Tap your name at the top of Setup to edit name, email, or birthday. Fields are labeled. Tap **Birthday**, pick a date, then **Save profile**.

## Theme

Toggle **Muted dark** for the forest canvas with forest-surface cards, cream-sage photos and chips, and light sage accents (same pairing as Your Journey).

## PIN and auto-lock

On **Manage profile**, under **PIN and auto-lock**, enter a **four-digit PIN** in the four boxes. It saves when you type the fourth digit. If a PIN is already saved, entering four digits replaces it.

When you open Slow Journey again, **Welcome back** sits in the middle of the screen with your first name. Use the on-screen number pad (not the phone keyboard) to enter the same four digits. The app unlocks as soon as the last box is filled. If today has no intentions yet and the day is still open, **Today's Intentions** opens next. The greeting matches the time of day. A wrong PIN shows an error and clears the boxes so you can try again. In landscape, the PIN boxes sit on one side and the number pad on the other so both stay visible.

With a PIN saved:

- Slow Journey asks for it when you fully close the app and open it again.
- **Auto-lock** is optional. Turn it on, then choose **2, 5, or 10 minutes**. If you leave Slow Journey (switch apps or hide it) for that long, it asks for your PIN when you come back. A short switch does not lock. Auto-lock stays off until a PIN exists.
- **Clear PIN** asks you to confirm, then removes the lock and turns auto-lock off.

The PIN stays on this device only. There is no cloud reset in Phase 1.

## Wipe

**Wipe local data** is listed with a short warning. It removes reflections, intentions, counters, and your profile. This cannot be undone.
