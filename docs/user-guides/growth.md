# Growth

Growth is computed on this device from days you completed.

You will see:

- Total reflections, current streak, longest streak
- A weekly bar view of evenings completed (week labels and counts; empty until you complete evenings)
- **Key learning** tags, strongest first, with how many evenings used that theme. Preset themes start you off; you can add your own on the reflection form.
- A **monthly insight** after you have a few reflections

Nothing here is uploaded in Phase 1.
