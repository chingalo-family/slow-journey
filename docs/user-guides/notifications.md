# Notifications

Reminders are invitations on this phone. They are never sent through a server.

## Open reminder settings

1. Go to **Setup**.
2. Tap **Notifications**.

The first time you open this screen with a reminder on, the phone asks for permission. Tap **Allow**. Slow Journey then writes the daily schedule.

If you previously declined, iOS will not ask again. Open **Settings → Slow Journey → Notifications** and turn them on, then return to this screen.

## Plan My Day / Reflect & Celebrate

- Turn each reminder on or off.
- Tap **Change time** to pick a clock time (defaults are **07:00** and **21:00**).
- Morning invitations motivate a gentle start (three intentions). Evening invitations ask you to look back, celebrate, and rest. The words change from day to day.

## See one in the notification list

Tap **Send a test notification**, then switch to the Home Screen. About eight seconds later a **morning-style** invitation should appear (the wording is chosen at random from the morning pool).

Daily reminders also show when Slow Journey is open. At the clock time you chose, look at the banner and the notification list (swipe down from the top).

Reminders survive a phone restart on Android. Coming back to the app also refreshes the schedule.
