# Notifications

Reminders are invitations on this phone. They are never sent through a server.

## Open reminder settings

1. Go to **Setup**.
2. Tap **Notifications**.

The first time you finish setup (or return to **Feed**), Slow Journey asks the phone for notification permission. Tap **Allow** to get **Plan My Day** around **7:00 AM** and **Reflect & Celebrate** around **9:00 PM**. You can change those times or turn either reminder off under **Setup → Notifications**.

If you previously declined, iOS will not ask again. Open **Settings → Slow Journey → Notifications** and turn them on, then return to Slow Journey.

## Plan My Day / Reflect & Celebrate

- Turn each reminder on or off.
- Tap **Change time** to pick a clock time (defaults are **7:00 AM** and **9:00 PM**).
- Morning invitations motivate a gentle start (three intentions). Evening invitations ask you to look back, celebrate, and rest. The words change from day to day.

Daily reminders show when Slow Journey is open. At the clock time you chose, look at the banner and the notification list (swipe down from the top).

Reminders survive a phone restart on Android. Coming back to the app also refreshes the schedule.
